# Meal Timing, Satiety & Health Principles

## Protein Feeding Frequency & Distribution
- **Distribution Model:** While total daily protein intake is the most critical variable, distributing protein intake across 3 to 5 evenly spaced meals (approximately every 3 to 5 hours) provides repeated pulses of essential amino acids and leucine to stimulate Muscle Protein Synthesis (MPS) throughout the 24-hour cycle.
- **Per-Meal Dose:** An intake of approximately 0.40 to 0.55 g/kg per meal (typically 25-45g) is sufficient to maximize the anabolic response in a single feeding.

## Dietary Fiber & Satiety
- **Minimum Daily Fiber:** An optimal target is 25 to 35 grams of total dietary fiber per day, or approximately 14 grams per 1,000 kcal consumed.
- **Physiological Benefits:** Soluble fiber delays gastric emptying, attenuates postprandial glucose spikes, and promotes feelings of fullness through gastric distention and peptide YY (PYY) release. Insoluble fiber promotes intestinal peristalsis and bowel regularity.
- **Microbiome Health:** Prebiotic dietary fibers nourish short-chain fatty acid (SCFA) producing colonic bacteria, enhancing systemic metabolic and immunological health.

## Hydration & Micronutrient Density
- **Fluid Requirements:** Baseline water requirements are approximately 35 to 45 ml per kg of body weight, increasing with ambient temperature and exercise sweat rate.
- **Whole-Food Baseline:** The 80/20 rule: obtaining at least 80% of daily calories from minimally processed, nutrient-dense whole foods (lean meats, fish, eggs, whole grains, tubers, vegetables, fruits, nuts, and healthy oils) ensures complete coverage of essential vitamins, minerals, and polyphenols.
